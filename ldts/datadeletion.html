Data Deletion Request Instructions

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

This document explains how users of Liar's Dice: Tavern Showdown ("the App"), developed by EnigmatosisGaming, can request the deletion of their user data in accordance with Google Play's User Data and Data Deletion policies.

1. Overview of Data Associated with the App

EnigmatosisGaming prioritizes player privacy. We do not require an EnigmatosisGaming user account, nor do we host personal player records on private servers. Data associated with the App falls into the following categories:

  • Local Gameplay Data & Preferences: Saved game states, sound and rule settings, and local statistics are stored exclusively on your device storage via local preferences.
  • Google Play Games Services (Optional): If you choose to sign in with your Google Play Games account, your Gamer ID, achievements, and cloud game saves are managed by Google.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Anonymous crash diagnostics, stack traces, and device hardware/OS specifications are processed via Google Firebase to troubleshoot errors and improve performance.
  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID), coarse location estimates, and ad performance metrics are processed by Google AdMob to serve advertisements.
  • Google Play Console Metrics: Standard app distribution metrics (install counts, uninstalls, crash clusters) collected in aggregate by Google Play without identifying individual users.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

Depending on the type of data, you can delete your data immediately via your device settings or submit a request directly to us.

Method A: Email Developer Request (Direct Support)

To request deletion of any developer-accessible logs or data associated with your interactions, send an email to:

Email: EnigmatosisGaming@gmail.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request - Liar's Dice: Tavern Showdown

Please include the following information to help us identify any relevant records:

  • Your Google Play Games Gamer ID or profile name (if signed in).
  • A description of the data you would like deleted.
  • Approximate date/time of any support or bug correspondence, if applicable.

Processing Time: We acknowledge requests within 5 business days and complete all verified deletion actions within 30 calendar days.

Method B: Immediate Self-Service Deletion (Device & Google Account)

You can instantly delete or sever all data using the following controls:

  1. Delete Local Game Data:
    • Open your Android device's Settings.
    • Navigate to Apps → Liar's Dice: Tavern Showdown → Storage & cache.
    • Tap Clear storage (or Clear data). This immediately wipes all local saves, game settings, and local records.
    • Uninstalling the App will also remove all locally stored app data.
  2. Delete Google Play Games Data:
    • Open the Google Play Games app on your Android device.
    • Tap the three vertical dots (menu) in the upper-right corner → Settings.
    • Scroll to Your data and tap Delete Play Games account & data.
    • Under "Delete individual game data", find Liar's Dice: Tavern Showdown and tap Delete.
  3. Delete or Reset Google Advertising ID (AdMob):
    • Open your device's Settings.
    • Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
    • Tap Delete advertising ID to remove the persistent advertising identifier used for ad personalization.

3. What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Deleted Upon Request

  • Any correspondence, support tickets, and associated contact information.
  • Direct developer associations between your Gamer ID and test or feedback logs.
  • All local application data upon executing the local clear storage procedure.

Data Retained & Exceptions

  • Aggregated & Anonymized Metrics: Anonymized performance metrics (such as aggregate crash statistics or total installation counters on Google Play Console) cannot be tied to an individual player and may be retained for general quality assurance.
  • Automatic Retention Limits by Third Parties: Diagnostic crash reports submitted through Firebase Crashlytics are anonymized and automatically purged by Google according to their standard retention schedule (typically within 90 days).
  • Legal & Security Compliance: Records required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or resolving ongoing legal disputes may be retained as permitted under applicable law.
