This Privacy Policy explains how EnigmatosisGaming ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application Liar's Dice: Tavern Showdown (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in privacy and simplicity. EnigmatosisGaming does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number). The App does not require user registration or account creation.

All gameplay data, custom game configurations, preferences, and saved game states are stored exclusively locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the free development of this App, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, and system language)
  • Unique device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location data derived from IP addresses
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

For more information on how Google uses data collected through advertising partner apps, please visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Managing and Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can control or reset your advertising preferences and opt out of personalized advertisements directly through your Android device settings:

  • Open your device's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID, or toggle on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Children's Privacy

Liar's Dice: Tavern Showdown is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
